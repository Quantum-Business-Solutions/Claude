Fleet table, as it would sit on a company record

Every machine at one account with its meters and volumes, one row each, expandable to the meter detail. Same component works as a HubSpot card or a panel in QuoteCommand — pick the account to see three different shapes of real data.

The design decision worth arguing about

Most of these machines have no volume at all, and the table says so with a dash rather than a 0. That is deliberate: a zero reads as "this machine prints nothing", and sizing a fleet from that is wrong in the expensive direction. Unknown and idle are different facts and should not look alike.

Where a rolling average is missing but the manufacturer's rated volume exists, the number shown is the rating, flagged rated. It is an honest substitute — a real number about the device rather than a measurement of this customer's usage.

What each column is

B/W, Color, Total come from separate meters, not one field. Total Count is e-automate's own all-clicks meter and is normally the sum of the others — so the columns should reconcile, and when they do not, that is information. Meters is the count on that device; expand any row with more than one to see all of them, including the types that carry nothing.

Where this could live

As a HubSpot UI extension it goes in the middle column of the company record — you already have the project at hubspot-app/ with the crm.objects.custom.read scope it needs. As a QuoteCommand panel it is a plain React table over the same data, and each row can carry a "quote this" action, which a HubSpot card cannot do as cleanly.
