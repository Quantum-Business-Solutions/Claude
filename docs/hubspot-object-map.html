01You need six custom-object slots, not twelve

The custom-object budget was the worry. It shouldn't be — five of the twelve map to standard HubSpot objects that cost nothing against the cap, and three of the remaining six already exist in this portal.

The arithmetic

Free: Customer→Company, SalesOrder→Deal, ServiceCall→Ticket, Item→Product, PrintReleaf→Company. Standard objects, no slot consumed.

Already built: Equipment 2-50535052, Contract 2-36237359, Lease 2-50535055.

To create: three. Order Line, Invoice, Invoice Line — and all three are rank 9 or lower, so a constrained portal can simply not have them.

On this DEVELOPER_TEST portal the cap isn't 10 anyway: it already holds 10 and an eleventh created fine (HTTP 201, deleted straight after). Verify against the production tier before relying on that.

03Three things that change the scope

Contact sync cannot be built — rank 12 is blocked, not deferred

All four Contact routes return CustomerContact, a join record, with the nested contact object null. You learn which contact links to which customer and nothing more — no name, email or phone. Claims_Contacts_Read is true, so it isn't permissions.

Do not put contact sync in a statement of work until CEO Juice explains why the nested objects aren't hydrated.

A human must create the e-automate account before a push

PUT /api/Customer and PUT /api/Contact/Create both return 500, even with only their one required field. ID634 validates against an existing customer and holds the order otherwise. So the account has to exist in e-automate before a quote can be pushed, and we can't put it there. Design the flow around that.

Measured volume doesn't exist; rated volume does

avgMonthlyVolume3Mo/6Mo/12Mo/Install read 0.0 on all 179 meter definitions — no exceptions — and there's no reading-history route to recompute them from. But mfgSuggestedMonthlyVolume and targetMonthlyVolume are populated on 32, across ABC Company, XYZ Company and Chase Bank - Houston.

So size fleets from rated capacity and configured target, and never render a zero average as "no printing".
