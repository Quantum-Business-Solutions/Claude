## Why this exists — the strategic reframe

Brand Command shows what the SEO data alone hid: **Aztec Office is a Managed Print Services (MPS) provider**, not a scanner e-commerce store. Their money is in fleet optimization, IT relief, print security and workflow contracts sold to **IT Directors, CFOs, and Office/Operations Managers at 25–500-employee firms** across legal, healthcare, accounting, education, municipal and logistics verticals.

But their **entire search/AEO footprint is transactional product terms** — "best document scanner," "hp414a toner," "canon copier." Those attract researchers and buyers of a $200 cartridge; they do **not** attract the IT Director evaluating a managed-print contract.

**The AEO opportunity is to build question trees for the MPS buyer's journey** — the questions those decision-makers actually ask AI engines — while keeping the transactional layer that already ranks. Each tree below is organized by Aztec's own **content pillars** and mapped to buyer, funnel stage, the answer asset, schema, and the reusable Flow that produces it (`playbooks/aeo-flows.md`).

**How to read a tree:** `Root` = the head question a buyer (and an AI engine) starts from → `Branches` = how the question decomposes (PAA / follow-ups) → `Leaves` = snippet-ready long-tail. `[SV n]` = Semrush-verified US volume. `[target]` = high-value AEO target, volume to be verified (Flow 6).
