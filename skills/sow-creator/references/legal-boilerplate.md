## SECTION 12 — CONFIDENTIALITY

### 12.1 Definition of Confidential Information

For purposes of this SOW, "Confidential Information" means any non-public, proprietary,
or confidential information disclosed by either Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other
Party ("Receiving Party"), whether disclosed orally, visually, electronically, or in writing,
including but not limited to:

- Business, financial, technical, operational, and strategic information
- Customer, prospect, employee, or vendor data
- Pricing, proposals, statements of work, and contractual terms
- System configurations, workflows, automation logic, data structures, and reporting
- HubSpot portal data, credentials, access information, and integrations
- The Q2™ framework, methodologies, templates, configurations, documentation, and
  intellectual property [Remove Q2™ reference if not applicable]
- Any information marked as "confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand
  to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure

Confidential Information includes information disclosed before or after the Effective Date
of this SOW.

### 12.2 Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

(a) Use Confidential Information solely for purposes of performing or receiving services
    under this SOW;
(b) Protect the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses
    to protect its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care;
(c) Restrict disclosure of Confidential Information to employees, contractors, or advisors
    who have a legitimate need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less
    protective than those contained herein;
(d) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written
    consent of the Disclosing Party, except as expressly permitted under this SOW.

### 12.3 Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this SOW;
(b) Was lawfully known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party;
(c) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the
    Confidential Information; or
(d) Is lawfully received from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation.

### 12.4 Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or court order to disclose Confidential
Information, the Receiving Party shall (to the extent legally permitted):

(a) Provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party; and
(b) Cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party in seeking protective treatment or
    limiting the scope of disclosure.

Only the portion of Confidential Information legally required to be disclosed may be disclosed.

### 12.5 Data Access & System Confidentiality

Each Party acknowledges that performance under this SOW may require access to systems,
software platforms, data, and credentials belonging to the other Party. Such access is
granted solely for purposes of performing obligations under this SOW and shall be treated
as Confidential Information.

Neither Party shall attempt to access systems, data, or information beyond the scope
expressly authorized under this SOW.

### 12.6 Intellectual Property Protection

Client acknowledges that [Quantum's proprietary frameworks, including Q2™,] and all related
methodologies, templates, configurations, and documentation constitute Confidential Information
and proprietary intellectual property of Quantum Business Solutions.

Client agrees not to copy, reverse engineer, distribute, sublicense, or disclose Quantum's
proprietary materials or configurations outside its organization without Quantum's prior
written consent.

### 12.7 Survival

The obligations of confidentiality set forth in this Section shall survive termination or
expiration of this SOW for a period of two (2) years, except with respect to trade secrets
and proprietary intellectual property, which shall be protected for so long as such information
remains confidential under applicable law.

### 12.8 Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure or misuse of Confidential Information
may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages may be insufficient. The Disclosing
Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive or equitable relief, in addition to any other
remedies available at law or equity.

## SECTION 13 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Quantum's total liability arising under or related to this SOW shall not exceed the total
fees paid by Client to Quantum under this SOW during the twelve (12) months immediately
preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

In no event shall either Party be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential,
exemplary, or punitive damages, including but not limited to loss of revenue, loss of profits,
loss of data, or loss of business opportunity, regardless of the theory of liability (contract,
tort, negligence, strict liability, or otherwise) and whether or not such Party has been
advised of the possibility of such damages.

Quantum provides configuration, enablement, and advisory services only. Business outcomes,
revenue generation, pipeline performance, system uptime, and results derived from the use
of any platform or configuration are not within Quantum's control and are not guaranteed
under this SOW.

## GENERAL LEGAL NOTES FOR SOW DRAFTING

1. **Always include limitation of liability** — without it, Quantum has unlimited exposure
2. **Always include IP protection** — state clearly who owns what
3. **Always include the Change Order requirement** — this is Quantum's primary scope protection
4. **Always include the seat-based fee non-cancellable language** — this is revenue protection
5. **Never guarantee outcomes** — frame everything as configuration and enablement
6. **Always use "Estimated Execution Time"** — never "prepaid hours" or "hour bank"
7. **If there is an MSA**, reference it in the header but do not restate all MSA terms
8. **If there is no MSA**, note that this SOW serves as the standalone agreement
