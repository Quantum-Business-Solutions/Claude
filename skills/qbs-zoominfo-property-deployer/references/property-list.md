## Notes

- `zoominfo_workflow_name` appears on both contacts AND companies — intentional, ZoomInfo uses it on both objects
- `management_level` and `zoominfo_management_level` both exist on contacts — the former is a legacy/standard field, the latter is ZoomInfo-specific
- Revenue range and employee range are **strings** (not numbers) — ZoomInfo pushes formatted ranges like "$10M-$50M"
- `founded_year` is a **string** — ZoomInfo pushes it as a 4-digit string
- All date fields: type=`date`, fieldType=`date`
- `zoominfo_contact_accuracy_score_` and `zoominfo_contact_accuracy_score_` are **numbers** (0-100 score)
- Department budgets are **numbers** (raw dollar amounts)
- Growth rates are **numbers** (percentage as decimal, e.g. 0.15 = 15%)
