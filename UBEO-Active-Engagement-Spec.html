Active Engagement: build spec

A proposal, not a deployment. It defines what counts as engagement, the exact properties to create, how each is calculated, and the order of work — and it flags the four properties already written to 749 company records that this replaces.

Why this needs a rewrite, not a patch

A first version is live on 749 companies. It defined meaningful engagement as an email reply or a meeting, and it was wrong on three counts:

  • It ignored phone calls. A logged call where somebody talked is engagement, and for this team it is the primary channel. 28% of the accounts labelled "NO TWO-WAY ENGAGEMENT" had a real conversation on record.
  • It counted the wrong things as touches. 52% of the 34,351 total were tasks, and roughly four in five tasks are dialer bookkeeping ("LVM", "no answer").
  • It read passive signals as activity. Half the accounts sampled had EMAIL_OPEN as their last activity — the prospect's mail client, often a security scanner, not anyone doing anything.

Under the corrected definition, 745 of 749 verdicts change. That is a replacement, so it is worth agreeing the definition first.

The definition

Three tiers. The middle one is where the flattery lives — a dashboard that counts "connected" calls as engagement will show an account as worked when every call was a receptionist saying he is not in.

Tier 1 — Attempt

Dialed, nobody reached: voicemail, no answer, IVR, closed office, failed connection. Also every dialer task stub.

84.0% of all 34,354 engagements at the target accounts.

Tier 2 — Contact made

A human answered and nothing came of it. A gatekeeper took a message; a flat "not interested" with nothing behind it. The phone connected. That is all it means.

6.1% of engagements. 13 accounts have never got past this tier.

Tier 3 — Substantive · this is Active Engagement

They told us something we did not already know, or a next step was agreed: their lease, vendor, equipment or timing; who decides; a callback, a meeting, or send-me-information.

10.0% — 3,426 in total. Inbound emails and held meetings count here by definition, without any text test.

Measured across every object, not just calls. An earlier pass read calls only — 32% of the record — and undercounted badly. Tasks carry the richest rep writing in this portal and produced 2,421 substantive records against the calls' 591.

A brush-off only counts as Tier 3 if it carries information. "Not interested" is Tier 2. "Not interested, we have two years left on the lease" is Tier 3 — it just gave us the date.

Why call outcome alone will not do this

91% of UBEO's 1.5M calls carry an outcome, so it is tempting to key off it. Checking the outcome values against what the notes actually say:

73a0d17f… 860,755 calls 11% are conversations

b2cf5968… 261,290 calls 8% are conversations

9d9162e7… 32,212 calls 40% are conversations

a4c4c377… 27,388 calls 94% are conversations

a091f579… 7,884 calls 97% are conversations

The dominant outcome, on 860,755 calls, is 89% voicemail. Only two values are reliable, covering ~35,000 calls. Outcome is a useful input, not the answer — and it must be verified per portal, never assumed from HubSpot's defaults.

Schema — and what actually needs AI

Most of this is native HubSpot mechanics and should be built that way: it is self-maintaining, auditable by the client, and does not depend on us re-running anything. Only one thing in the whole design genuinely requires reading text.

Three of the seven properties originally proposed here were duplicating these. They are dropped.

Layer 2 — native build, no AI

Workflows and calculated properties. This is the Active Engagement Package pattern, and it covers the large majority of the design.

PropertyObjectMechanism — no AI
Most Recent Conversation Datecontact Workflow. Enroll on call created where hs_call_disposition is one of the two verified connected outcomes; stamp the date. 91% of calls carry an outcome, so this populates far more than the 1.8% the reply field reaches today.
Most Recent Email Reply Datecontact Workflow. Enroll on email created where hs_email_direction = INCOMING. A reply is a reply — no text analysis.
Most Recent Meeting Datecontact Copy of hs_latest_meeting_activity, or a workflow on meeting outcome if past-vs-booked needs separating.
Active Engagement Datecontact Calculated property. MAX of the three above. This is the headline field.
Time Since Active Engagementcontact Calculated property. Days since. The segmentation field.
Active Engagement Date / Time Sincecompany Sync properties. Roll the contact values to the company, as the existing package does.

The only field that needs AI

A call outcome tells you the phone connected. It cannot tell you whether anything was said. Separating Tier 2 from Tier 3 — a receptionist taking a message versus a prospect disclosing their lease, naming who decides, or agreeing a next step — requires reading the note. Nothing native can do it.

PropertyObjectWhy AI
ai__engagement_tiercontact / company Substantive / Contact only / Attempts only / Never touched. The substance judgement.
ai__engagement_overviewcompany The plain-language read a rep acts on, with attempts, contacts and conversations stated separately.
ai__engagement_verdictcompany Combines the tier with decision-maker state and lease timing — cross-object, so no single workflow produces it.

Three AI fields, not seven. Everything else is native and maintains itself.

Consequence worth noting

Layer 2 is worth building on its own merits even if the AI layer is never deployed. UBEO currently has an engagement signal on 7.5% of contacts, and the two fields that mean anything real sit at 1.8% and 1.2%. The conversation workflow alone would change that, because the input it keys on is present on 91% of calls.

Deployment order

  1. Agree the definition above. Nothing else matters if Tier 3 is drawn in the wrong place.
  2. Retire v1. Four properties are live on 749 companies under the old definition. Clear them before writing, so no record briefly carries both.
  3. Dry run on 25 accounts spanning all five verdicts. Read every one by hand against its timeline before any bulk write.
  4. Deploy to the 749 accounts with a lease inside 12 months.
  5. QA gates below, then extend to the full 2,676 with a lease date.
  6. Convert to workflows so it maintains itself.

QA gates, each blocking

  • No date renders as a negative interval — booked meetings are in the future.
  • No account is described as having no conversation when a Tier 3 call exists on it.
  • Attempts, contacts and conversations are reported separately in every overview.
  • A passive signal is never described as activity.
  • Sample 15 records by hand against the HubSpot timeline. Every earlier defect in this engagement was found this way and none by aggregate checks.
  • Hand-verify the task rule before trusting it. Tasks produced 2,421 substantive records and drove "engaged" to 87% of accounts, well above the ~45% ceiling originally assumed. Either the assumption was wrong or the rule is loose on tasks — read 30 substantive tasks against their timelines and settle it before any deploy.
