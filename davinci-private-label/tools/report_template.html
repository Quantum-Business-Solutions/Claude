How to read this

The three number columns are desktop, tablet and phone. A dash means that width has not been measured yet — the sixteen category pages were checked by an older tool that only ever looked at desktop.

Two figures that are not what they look like

Home cannot be judged yet. Its reference render is missing the page's own stylesheet, so the comparison blames the rebuilt page for laying out grids correctly. Home does separately have real defects — a dropped link and a dropped image — which are being worked.

Section-level pixel percentages are not listed here because on pages whose rebuild merges two original sections into one module, that number measures the merge rather than any regression, and cannot reach zero however good the page is.

Still moving

Draft pages are being measured and fixed continuously, so these figures are a snapshot. Nothing is published, every original keeps its slug, and no original is altered until its rebuild measures clean.
