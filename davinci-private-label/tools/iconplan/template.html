Before any of this deploys

  1. Barb is still working through the copy. 67 ™ marks, 22 ® marks and 18 visitor-facing DaVinci references are still live, several on pages that also carry icons. Confirm which pages she has finished before writing to them.
  2. Two icons have a stale twin in the library. The Fitness runner went up as fitness-green-64 and the redrawn Prenatal as prenatal-care-green-1, with the superseded versions still sitting beside them. Ten files should be deleted so a filename match cannot pick the wrong one.
  3. Three icons are much heavier than the rest. Measured ink coverage inside the artwork: senior 63%, us-manufacturing 62% and the redrawn prenatal-care, against roughly 32% for a typical stroke icon. They will read as bolder than everything around them.
  4. Immune and Foundation are both shields. Sarah has rejected duplicate icons twice already — Muscle/Strength, and Design Your Brand/Product Selection.
  5. U.S. Manufacturing will not read at 28px, the size the cards use. Its own floor is 32px.
  6. Hormonal Balance is Mindy’s call. Sarah prefers the linked male/female pair; that glyph is the established intersex symbol and will be read that way.
