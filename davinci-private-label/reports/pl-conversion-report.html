What “measured clean” means

Every word of the original present and nothing invented; no link or image dropped; no visible difference in type size, weight, colour or column width at 1440, 768 or 390 px; and the FAQ accordions behaving the same. The checker was itself tested before each run by planting a 2 px change it had to catch — a gate that has never failed on purpose is not known to work.

Where a page differs on purpose

A note in the last column means the rebuild departs from the original and was reviewed and accepted — usually because the original laid out badly on a phone, holding three columns where the rebuild stacks them. Those differences are written down rather than waved through, so an accepted difference is never mistaken for no difference.
