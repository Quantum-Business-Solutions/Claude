## Why it exists

Tammy raised it on the 2 September call, asking where the assets she had prepared had gone:

> "So, real quick, where I... we had created some bottle images for our top sellers, and some
> sell sheets. Where... where and what did we do with that?"

Patrick, on the 3 September huddle with Mindy, put it plainly:

> "Tammy mentioned yesterday, she's like, what else do you guys need to **create our page with
> our top 10 products**, and I reminded her that she hasn't shown us a layout that she wants.
> So she's gonna follow through with that."

So the page is wanted, and the **layout is still owed by the client**. A placeholder holds the
slot without guessing at a design she has already said she will supply.

## What is in it

Cloned from `sell-sheets` (216194811689) - same template, same shape, closest existing
analogue. Then:

- Hero reworded, and states in plain terms that it is a placeholder awaiting her layout and assets
- **Ten empty product slots**, each with name, one-line benefit and an image field, alt text
  already written as "Product N bottle image - to be supplied"
- `min_column_width` set to 340 so ten items lay out 3+3+3+1 rather than the module default of
  four across. Same fix applied to the category grids the same day
- FAQ block removed - nothing to answer on a placeholder
- Canonical set to its own URL

## Scope note

Both Patrick and Shawn flagged this class of work on the 3 September call as outside the
original engagement - Patrick: *"there are things that are trickling in that weren't really
part of the scope."* Recorded here because a placeholder is cheap, but building out ten
products of copy and imagery is not, and that should be a deliberate commercial decision
rather than something that quietly happens.
