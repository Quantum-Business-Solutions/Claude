Top Private Label Protein Powders for Your Brand

Protein powders are popular health products that provide many benefits to consumers. Yet, standing out in a crowded market is a challenge. UNDERSTANDING THE BASICS OF PROTEIN POWDER

Protein powder is a nutritional supplement that enhances exercise performance and recovery and helps build muscle mass. Products are often categorized by the source of protein and the processing and concentration of protein.

For example, protein powders may be classified as concentrate, isolate, and hydrolysate from protein sources such as:

Whey
Casein
Egg albumin
Pea 
Soy
Beef

Protein processing extracts the protein from its source, removing most of its fiber, fat, carbs, and minerals. A highly filtered protein is called protein isolate, with 90% protein and fewer other nutrients, while a protein concentrate has 70% to 80% protein and higher amounts of other nutrients. Protein hydrolysate contains smaller components, such as peptides and amino acids, that may improve absorption. 

Manufacturers sometimes add other ingredients in the final stages to enhance flavor and nutrition, increase shelf life, and maintain quality. These include:

Vitamins 
Minerals
Sugars
Artificial flavorings
Thickeners
HOW DOES PRIVATE LABELING WORK FOR PROTEIN POWDER?

Private-label protein powders are supplements a third-party vitamin supplier produces, but you sell under your brand. The manufacturer creates specific protein powder formulations, and you choose the formulation, add your branding, and sell the product. 

Private labeling is a standard business practice and a popular choice for many reasons. For one, you can add high-quality products to your retail line without opening a manufacturing facility or navigating supply chain challenges. 

In addition, a private-label protein powder is more cost-effective. Manufacturers have the facilities, training, and certifications to produce supplements, which translates to lower costs and higher profits. 

THE GROWING TRENDS IN PROTEIN POWDER PRODUCTION

As the wellness industry grows, protein powders have become popular. In 2024 alone, the protein supplement market was valued at $7.2 million and will expand to $14.7 million by 2033, a growth rate of 8.3%.

The demand comes from health-conscious individuals who focus on healthy nutrition and protein for weight management, performance, muscle growth, and overall health. At the same time, vegan lifestyles are on the upward swing, increasing the demand for plant-based, ingredient-conscious, and allergen-friendly products. 

However, finding products that meet these demands can be challenging. Private-label protein powder manufacturers can identify market trends and top-selling products, helping you determine the best supplements for your business.  

5 TOP PROTEIN POWDERS YOUR BRAND SHOULD FEATURE

There are many forms of protein powder, but some are more popular than others. These top five are most in-demand. 

1. Vegan Protein

Vegan protein powders are plant-based products that contain no animal products. These supplements are nutrient-dense, with more fiber and less fat than animal-based proteins. In addition, plant-based or vegan proteins are easy to digest, may help promote muscle growth, and support strength, endurance, normal recovery, and healthy weight. 

2. Whey Protein

Whey protein powder comes from milk and contains eight types of milk proteins. Whey protein has different amounts of lactose and fat, depending on whether it's an isolate, hydrolysate, or concentrate. However, whey is a good source of essential amino acids, helps support nutrition, and supports muscle maintenance. 

3. Paleo-Inspired Protein

Paleo-inspired protein supplements fit into the Paleolithic diet, which includes meat, eggs, seafood, fruits, vegetables, tubers, nuts, and seeds and excludes dairy, grains, and processed foods.

Paleo-inspired supplements often come from animal proteins, such as beef or egg. These products contain essential amino acids and may help with healthy weight management, muscle maintenance, and protein, mineral, and vitamin intake. 

4. Keto-Inspired Protein

The ketogenic or keto diet follows a high-fat, high-protein, and low-carb eating pattern. The combination promotes weight loss, increased energy, and mental clarity. Keto-inspired supplements may be any protein source, as long as the final product has low carb and low sugar content. 

5.  Hemp Protein

Hemp protein powders are vegan products with protein sourced from the seeds of the Cannabis sativa plant, called hemp. Hemp supplements are rich in healthy fats, fiber, and added vitamins and minerals. 

In addition, hemp protein supplements are a good source of essential amino acids, helping boost your protein intake and overall nutrition. These supplements promote healthy weight, help build muscle, and may be easier to digest than whey or other animal-based proteins.
