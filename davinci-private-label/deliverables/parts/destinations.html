Can an email link to a draft page?

No. An unpublished HubSpot page 404s at its live URL, and the draft is only reachable behind an hs_preview key that is neither public-safe nor stable. The workable order is the reverse: write the final praxerasupplements.com URL into the email now and hold the email in draft until the page publishes. The link starts working the moment the page goes live, and there is nothing to rewrite afterwards.
