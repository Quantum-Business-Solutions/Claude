The hard half of the question

Private label, or DaVinci’s own brand?

Ruling out PetTechLabs is the easy half. The hard half is that DaVinci sells supplements under its own name to practitioners and runs a private-label business, from one portal, on one contact list, and the phrase “private label” appears on both sides.

So nothing here is classified by topic. It is classified by namespace — the address the asset actually lives at:

  • Private label — the asset sits at a private-label address: the /private-label/ blog group, an info.davincilabs.com/private-label* slug, the /private-labeling* main-site paths, pl-demo-*, or praxerasupplements.com. 150 blog posts qualify this way.
  • DaVinci brand that links to private label — a brand asset with one incidental private-label link. It needs its URL updated at cutover and nothing else. The “DV Mission & Register” emails are the clearest case: practitioner registration copy with a single link to /private-labeling.
  • Not private label at all — the cart-recovery and “Register for your account” product emails. They carry no private-label link whatsoever. Someone abandoning a cart with a discount code is buying DaVinci product; a private-label buyer commissions their own brand.

25 posts are about private label but live in another brand’s namespace

Every one of them is PetTechLabs — a separate brand in this same portal with its own private-label pet-supplement line. Topically they are private label. Structurally they are not DaVinci’s, and none should move to Praxera. This is the exact trap a name-based list falls into.
