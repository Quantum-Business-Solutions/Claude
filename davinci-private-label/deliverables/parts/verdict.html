The three things that decide the launch

1  Nothing on the new site is wired to anything

All six forms embedded across the 65 Praxera pages have zero live workflows listening to them. The nine live private-label workflows enrol on four different forms — PRIVATE LABEL NEW, PRIVATE LABEL NEW (New DV Site), Contact Us NEW, LP: Private Labeling Supplements Guide. A lead who fills in the new consultation form today receives nothing and reaches no queue.

Detected by matching form GUIDs found on the pages against enrolment criteria in every flow. A workflow enrolling by list membership or property change would not appear this way, so read this as “no form-triggered follow-up”, not a mathematical zero.

2  The two most-linked destinations aren’t in HubSpot

www.davincilabs.com/private-label-get-started is the target of 83 email links and 70 workflow references; /private-labeling/ takes another 27. Both live on the Magento main site, outside HubSpot, so no CMS-side redirect reaches them. They are the largest single breakage risk in the migration and they need a redirect owner outside this portal.

3  The guide funnel has no capture on the new site

Of the pages that offer a download, only pl-demo-onboarding-guide carries a form. pl-demo-guides, pl-demo-sell-sheets, pl-demo-ingredients-testing, pl-demo-definitive-guide and pl-demo-resources have none. A Praxera-branded Ingredients & Testing form was built on 26 July — it was never placed on a page.
