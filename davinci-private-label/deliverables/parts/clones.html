Two things could not be renamed

i_would_like_to_subscribe_to_the_davinci_blog and how_did_you_hear_about_davinci___other__please_specify_ are contact-property internal names, not form copy. They are shared with every other form and every contact record in the portal, so renaming them is a portal-wide change, not a rebrand task. The labels a visitor reads have been changed; the internal names have not.

Everything else came out: the privacyText consent block is stored per form and now says Praxera on all three forms that carried it, and every post-submit redirect now points at a Praxera thank-you page.
