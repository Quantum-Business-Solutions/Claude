## Proposed answer

> Private labeling means you launch a fitness product under your own brand using one of our pre-existing, doctor-formulated stock formulas. Quality testing and bottling are handled for you, and every product is made in an FDA-registered, cGMP-certified U.S. facility. You select the formulation from our catalog, design your label, and receive the finished product ready to ship under your brand. No formulation work, no setup fees, no lab build-out.
