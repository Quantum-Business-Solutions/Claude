# Using the Praxera asset sign-off (team note)

Open: https://clientcommand.thequantumleap.business/pages/94f31f00-08a7-4f9c-905e-fe4a5e6b8c7d
Send the client: https://clientcommand.thequantumleap.business/portal/2a5c361066202d71fa47ee598d2bcb95/pages/94f31f00-08a7-4f9c-905e-fe4a5e6b8c7d

1. Type your name and pick **I am QBS** (the client picks **I am the client**). Once the new
   ClientCommand viewer is live this happens automatically from your login.
2. Work a group (Website pages, Blog posts, Emails, Forms, Workflows). Use the filters:
   *Not reviewed*, *Has a finding*, *Open comments*, or the type dropdown (Core, Category…).
3. **QBS ✓** when the build is right. The client presses **Client ✓** when the content is right.
   **Needs work** if it is not — then say why in a comment.
4. **Comments**: open a row → the client's existing comments are there. Reply, then **Resolve ✓**
   when done. The sheet counts open comments per row.
5. **Findings**: chips on the row; open the row for the detail (which DaVinci URL, which phrase).
   Clear a chip with × once it is fixed.
6. **Edit / Add / Remove** rows as the asset list changes. Removed rows can be restored.
7. Tick several rows and use the bulk bar for approvals in one go.
8. **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet copy. **Activity** at the top shows who did what, when.

Everything saves to the portal automatically ("saved to the portal" top right). Two people can
work at the same time.

## What changed on 9 Sep (v2.3)
- Activity now sits at the top: who did what, when, newest first. "Show all" opens the full history.
- Header tiles include Open comments and Resolved. A resolved comment is struck through with a
  ✓ Resolved pill and "resolved by …"; Reopen brings it back.
- "↗ Make global" on a row comment copies it into Global notes & rules (site-wide items such as the
  manufacturing wording).
- "Collapse all / Expand all" in the sticky bar; every section also has its own ▾ icon.
- "Redirects from at cutover" is the real public DaVinci URL that will 301 to the page (click ↗ to see
  the old page). Pages built new for Praxera show "nothing redirects here".
