Found today. Both would embarrass the brand at launch.

Blocker Every error page on praxerasupplements.com serves the DaVinci Labs website

Type any wrong address on the Praxera domain and you do not get a Praxera page. You get the full DaVinci Labs storefront: their logo, their product menu, their footer, and 58 links straight to davincilabs.com. Its page title is empty and it is tagged author: DaVinci Laboratories.

The same is true of the member login page, titled DaVinci Laboratories, and the email preferences page, titled DaVinci Laboratories Email Services.

This defeats the point of the split. The whole reason for a separate brand was that a practitioner should not find their way from Praxera back to DaVinci. Right now a single typo does exactly that. These are domain-level system page settings, separate from the 63 pages, which is why page-by-page review never caught it.

Blocker The blog is already public, and it is wearing DaVinci's clothes

www.praxerasupplements.com/blog returns a live page right now, to anyone, before launch. It carries the DaVinci header and footer: Meet The DaVinci Team, DaVinci for Doctors, Stay up to date on all of the latest DaVinci products, and 58 outbound links to davincilabs.com.

The cause is a single empty setting. The blog's post template is correctly set to Praxera, but its listing template is blank, so the listing falls back to the DaVinci default. There are also zero Praxera posts published, so the page is an empty DaVinci-branded shell.

One template setting fixes the chrome. The posts are a separate question for the 14th.
