## CANNOT GO LIVE AS-IS

### 1. Dropshipping page — Tammy: *"We do not want to offer drop shipping."*

> *"This can't go live like this."*

This is not one sentence. The page is built on the service end to end:

- "We ship every order directly to your customer."
- "Dropshipping means we fulfill every order directly to your customer."
- "Built-in dropshipping infrastructure."
- "Partner with us for fast, reliable dropshipping."
- An entire FAQ block — fulfilment times, per-unit costs, international dropshipping
- The meta description sells it

Rewriting it as the informational page Patrick suggested (*"if you want to dropship your
supplements, here's how"*) is a **full rewrite**, not an edit. With 12 days, **hide it** is
the realistic call, and rewrite after launch.

**And it does not travel alone.** "dropship" also appears on **`our-process`** (1) and
**`resources`** (2) — the green *Dropship Private Label* button Tammy flagged. Those go with
the page.

### 2. Twenty "AND MORE" tiles are broken links

Tammy asked whether the AND MORE box was clickable. Patrick answered *"that's not linked
anywhere yet."* **It is linked — to `/pl-demo-pillar`, which does not exist on
praxerasupplements.com.** No Praxera slug contains "pillar" or "pl-demo".

So it is worse than unlinked: on **20 pages** it will 404. Every category page carries one,
plus home, fitness, dropshipping and how-to-sell.

The intended destination is almost certainly `learning/definitive-guide` (the old
`pl-demo-pillar-v3`). One find-and-replace across 20 pages.
