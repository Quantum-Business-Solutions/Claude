# Is "1976" anywhere? — Praxera and the old DaVinci site

**15 Sep 2026.** FoodScience has been in business since **1973** (Shawn). This checks whether a
wrong year is published anywhere, on Praxera or on the DaVinci property it was migrated from.

## Answer: no

There is no 1976 used as a date on either site. The one 1976 that exists is a body-weight
statistic on a DaVinci white paper, and it is correct.

| Property | What was checked | 1976 | 1973 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **www.praxerasupplements.com** | all 140 published pages and posts, rendered text | **0** | 6 |
| **Praxera marketing emails** | 219 Praxera-scope emails (of 3,091 in the shared portal) | **0** | 0 |
| **www.davincilabs.com** | 29 informational pages, rendered in Chromium | **0** | 2 |
| **blog.davincilabs.com** | all 474 URLs in the sitemap | **0** | 12 |
| **info.davincilabs.com** | 283 of 415 landing pages reachable | **1** | 1 |

### The one 1976

`info.davincilabs.com/weight-management-white-paper`:

> "…overweight has increased dramatically in the last few decades, going from **15% in 1976** to
> 32.9% in 2003."

A prevalence statistic, not a company date. Nothing to fix.

### Where 1973 appears, and it is right everywhere

- **Praxera** — `/about` ×4 ("Praxera was founded in 1973", "1973 founded in Vermont", "where the
  company has lived since 1973", "…since 1973"), `/quality-standards` ("50+ Years · producing
  Dietary supplements since 1973"), and `/blog/what-is-a-nutraceutical-supplement`.
- **DaVinci** — `www.davincilabs.com/our-company` ("50 YEARS OF COMMITMENT & KNOWLEDGE. Since
  1973, DaVinci® Laboratories has been…"), and 12 blog posts, including "1973 FoodScience®, LLC,
  the parent company…".

Praxera's wording matches DaVinci's. No discrepancy.

## A trap worth recording

A plain text search for "1976" returns **182 hits on the Praxera site pages alone** — and every
one is digits inside a HubSpot identifier, e.g. `module_id: 119762877841`. None of them is copy.
Anyone re-running this needs to require that the year is not wedged inside a longer number
(`(?<!\d)1976(?!\d)`) and to search rendered text rather than the serialised JSON, or they will
chase 182 ghosts.

## Coverage gaps, stated plainly

- 132 of the 415 `info.davincilabs.com` landing pages did not return a page — unpublished or
  retired. They were not read.
- Praxera **drafts** (capsules, chewables, powders, gummies, dropshipping, case-studies) are not
  in the live crawl. They are unpublished, so nothing wrong there is visible to anyone yet, but
  they should be re-checked before they publish.
- `www.davincilabs.com` is a Magento storefront outside this HubSpot portal. Product pages were
  excluded; only the 29 informational pages linked from the home page were read.

## Tools

- `tools/find_year_1976.py` — API sweep over pages, posts, emails, forms and theme source. Correct
  but slow: it reads every asset individually and times out against a 3,091-email portal. Use it
  scoped, or use the live crawl below for a whole-site answer.
- The live crawls used for this report are the faster route: pull the sitemap (or the page list
  from the API), fetch the rendered HTML in parallel, strip script/style, then match the year as a
  standalone number. `www.davincilabs.com` returns **403 to curl** and has to be read through
  Chromium.
