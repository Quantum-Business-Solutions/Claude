The Onboarding accreditation requires the profiled customer to hold both Marketing Hub and Sales Hub at Professional or above. CalcFocus holds Marketing Hub Professional and Sales Hub Professional — confirmed 12 August 2026.

The situation

CalcFocus, a 58-person software business in Philadelphia, had bought HubSpot and never implemented it. There was no trustworthy CRM baseline anywhere in the business.

Contact data sat in spreadsheets and disconnected lists. Pipeline data sat with individual sellers. The deal stages available in the tool did not describe how the company actually forecast revenue. There was no lifecycle model, no segmentation, no marketing infrastructure, and no reporting layer.

The engagement had to deliver a working revenue system across both Marketing Hub and Sales Hub, migrate the business's real data into it, and leave the team able to run it without us.

How the engagement was run

An eight-phase gated plan with explicit client-action dependencies and formal client confirmation at every hub boundary — objectives-based onboarding, not a feature tour.

What was built

Foundational architecture

  • Segmentation — Q2 Universal List Library, 111 new lists
  • Lifecycle — Q2 lifecycle stage workflows; "Former Customer" workflow remediated
  • Personas — persona properties plus a buyer persona workflow
  • Routing — round-robin lead assignment workflow
  • Attribution — Q2 Attribution and Sequence Package deployed via Supered
  • Integrations — LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Microsoft 365 mail and calendar

Marketing Hub

Email sending domain with DKIM/SPF/DMARC; four form templates (Contact Us, eBook, Webinar, Quote); nurture campaign framework; marketing dashboard; WordPress tracking code. Configuration was driven by three client questionnaires rather than assumption.

Sales Hub

Standard sales dashboard; deals pipeline and active lists; task queues; custom call outcomes, meeting types and meeting outcomes; calendar connections confirmed for all users.

Challenges and lessons learned

No trustworthy data baseline

Rather than import and correct afterwards, a joint verification step was gated into the plan and migrated deal data was routed to the sales owner for review before final import. A review gate before final import costs a week and saves a quarter of remediation.

The forecast model did not match the tool

Out-of-the-box deal stages did not reflect how CalcFocus forecast. Stages and probabilities were rebuilt to the client's own model. Where a business has a working forecast model, configure the CRM to it — adoption follows familiarity.

A capability gap at the licensed tier

Native churn reporting requires Enterprise; CalcFocus holds Professional. Instead of routing the requirement into a licence upgrade, workarounds were built with calculated properties. The first instinct on a tier gap should be engineering, not procurement.

Scope discovered mid-flight

A standing "Wish List — Items Identified Out of Scope During Onboarding" phase captured new requirements without destabilising the plan. Give mid-engagement scope somewhere legitimate to go and it stops being a conflict.

Outcome

CalcFocus moved from an unimplemented HubSpot instance to a live two-hub revenue system with migrated data, a 111-list segmentation library, lifecycle and persona automation, round-robin routing, attribution, marketing and sales dashboards, and a trained team — confirmed complete by the client at three separate gates.

The relationship converted from project to retainer. Weekly client success meetings and monthly strategic reviews are booked through December 2026, with 45 of 76 post-onboarding tickets already closed.
