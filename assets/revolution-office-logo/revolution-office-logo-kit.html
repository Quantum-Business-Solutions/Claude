Brand asset kit

Revolution Office, closed-circle lockup

The ring is now a complete circle. Every file below is a vector recreation with the text outlined, so it scales cleanly to a business card or a truck wrap. Fifteen colourways, three layouts, transparent and solid-ground versions of each.

Layout
Ground

Master

RingClosed circle, stroke 20 units on a 210 unit diameter. R sits optically centred, 2 units left of true centre.
TypeMontserrat ExtraBold for REVOLUTION and the R, Bold with 110 unit tracking for OFFICE, Medium for the tagline. Outlined to paths in every SVG.
ColourOrange #E8872B on ink #141414. Minimum clear space is the ring's stroke width on all sides.
